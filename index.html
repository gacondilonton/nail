Good to Know

Policy & Aftercare

Deposit Policy

A $20 NZD deposit is required at the time of booking to secure your appointment. The deposit will be deducted from your total service cost on the day.

Cancellation

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or changes. Late cancellations or no-shows will forfeit the deposit. We appreciate your understanding.

Nail Aftercare

Avoid hot water for 2 hours post-service. Wear gloves when cleaning. Apply cuticle oil daily for lasting results. If any lifting occurs within 7 days, pop in for a complimentary fix.
